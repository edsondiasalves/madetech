Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 03, 2026

Tugazenship is developed by MadeTech to provide a secure platform for tracking Portuguese citizenship applications. We value your privacy and are transparent about the data we collect.

1. Data Collection

We use Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts. When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your UID, name, and email address.

We store your citizenship process details (process access code, submission date, registry office, and citizenship article) in Firebase Firestore to sync your tracking experience across devices.

2. Advertising and Consent

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. To comply with privacy regulations (GDPR/LGPD), we use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect your consent before showing personalized ads.

3. Data Deletion

How to delete your data:

You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the Tugazenship app:

  1. Open the app menu or settings.
  2. Go to Privacy & Account.
  3. Click on Delete account and data.

Alternatively, you can visit our Data Removal Page or email us to request manual deletion.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or your data, please contact us at madetech39@gmail.com.
