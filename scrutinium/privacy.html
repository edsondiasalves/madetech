Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 02, 2026

Scrutinium is built to provide a platform for democratic polling and demographic insights. We value your privacy and are transparent about the data we collect.

1. Data Collection

We use Firebase Authentication to manage user accounts. When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your UID, name, and email address.

We store your votes, submitted questions, and profile preferences (gender, age range, education, etc.) in Firebase Firestore to sync your experience across devices.

2. Advertising and Consent

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. To comply with privacy regulations (GDPR/LGPD), we use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect your consent before showing personalized ads.

3. Data Deletion

How to delete your data:

You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the Scrutinium app:

  1. Open the app menu.
  2. Go to Privacy & Account.
  3. Click on Delete account and data.

This action will permanently remove your profile, voting history, and submitted questions from our active databases.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy or your data, please contact us at support@scrutinium.app.
