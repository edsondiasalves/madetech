Scrutinium Privacy Policy and Account Deletion

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This page explains how Scrutinium handles user data and how users can request deletion of their account and associated data. Replace the contact email below with your real support address before publishing this page.

Data we collect

When you sign in, Scrutinium may process your Firebase user ID, display name, email address, profile preferences, answers, votes, submitted questions, and app interaction records needed to provide the voting experience.

Why we use data

  • To authenticate users with Google, Microsoft, and Firebase Authentication.
  • To sync votes, answers, submitted questions, and profile preferences using Firebase Firestore.
  • To show aggregated voting and demographic results.
  • To show ads using Google AdMob after consent is handled through Google User Messaging Platform where required.

Third-party services

Scrutinium uses Firebase Authentication, Firebase Firestore, Google AdMob, and Google User Messaging Platform. These services may process device, app, advertising, diagnostic, or authentication data according to their own terms and policies.

Sharing and sale of data

Scrutinium does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers that operate authentication, cloud storage, ads, and consent infrastructure for the app.

Retention

Account-linked data is retained while the account is active. Aggregated anonymous statistics may be retained because they are not tied to a specific account.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account inside the app from Privacy & account while signed in. This removes your profile, answer history, submitted questions, and vote records associated with your user ID, then deletes your Firebase Authentication account.

You can also request deletion by email: privacy@example.com. Include the email address used to sign in to Scrutinium. Replace this email with your real support address before submitting to Google Play.
