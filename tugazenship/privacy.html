Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Tugazenship ("the App") is developed by MadeTech. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a secure experience for tracking your Portuguese citizenship application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The App collects and processes the following types of information:

  • Authentication Data: When you sign in using Google or Microsoft, we receive your basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) to identify your account and sync your data across devices.
  • Citizenship Process Data: You provide your process access code, submission date, and article. This data is used solely to fetch the current status from official IRN sources and calculate estimated completion dates.
  • Local Storage: Some data, like your consultation history, may be stored locally on your device for performance.

2. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication and secure database storage.
  • Official IRN Website: The app acts as a proxy to fetch your process status from the official IRN portal using your provided access code.
  • AdMob: To display non-intrusive advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve relevant ads.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is only shared with service providers (like Firebase) as necessary to operate the App.

4. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access and export your data.
  • Correct any inaccuracies.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time through the App settings or by visiting our Data Removal Page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at madetech39@gmail.com.
