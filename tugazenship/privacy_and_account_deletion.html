Tugazenship Privacy Policy and Account Deletion

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This page explains how Tugazenship handles user data and how users can request deletion of their account and associated citizenship process records.

Data we collect

When you sign in and use Tugazenship, we process your Firebase user ID, display name, email address, process access code, application submission date, assigned registry office, and citizenship article required to fetch IRN status and calculate progress estimates.

Why we use data

  • To authenticate users with Google, Microsoft, and Firebase Authentication.
  • To sync saved citizenship process tracking records using Firebase Firestore across your devices.
  • To fetch official IRN application progress stages using your provided access code.
  • To show ads using Google AdMob after consent is handled through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required.

Third-party services

Tugazenship uses Firebase Authentication, Firebase Firestore, Google AdMob, and Google User Messaging Platform. These services process device, app, advertising, diagnostic, or authentication data according to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Sharing and sale of data

Tugazenship does not sell personal data. Data is processed solely by infrastructure providers operating authentication, cloud database, ads, and consent services for the app.

Retention

Account-linked citizenship process data is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated tracking records are permanently removed.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account inside the app from Privacy & account while signed in. This removes your profile and saved process records from our active database, then deletes your Firebase Authentication account.

You can also request manual deletion by visiting our Account Deletion Page or by email: madetech39@gmail.com. Include the email address used to sign in to Tugazenship.
